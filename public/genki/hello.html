How I built thisVibe-coded with Claude

The prompt and the rules

You asked for a vibe-coded application, so here it is. It started with this prompt:

Can we create a vibe-coded job application page that screams digital nomad? Maybe a scroll-like experience with palm trees, and Europe and Asia as you scroll.

Then I steered it with a few rules:

  • Real places only. The van drives my actual route, Germany to Spain this winter. Asia appears as where your team is, not as a trip I invented.
  • Content comes from my CV, nothing made up.
  • Everything is readable without the animation, and the scene stays calm if you've turned on reduced motion.
  • Works on a phone, in light and dark mode, as a single file with no libraries.

Claude wrote the code. I chose the concept, supplied the content, and reviewed and edited the result before sending it.
